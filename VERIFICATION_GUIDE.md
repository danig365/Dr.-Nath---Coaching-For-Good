# Verification Guide — Platform Updates

A quick check for each completed update. First, **hard-refresh** the site
(Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R) so you're on the latest version.

---

### 1. All clients must log in
- Open any session **Join** link from an email (or the app).
- **Expect:** it asks you to log in first — no page opens without signing in.

### 2. Clients restricted to their programme + easy onboarding
- Admin → **Onboard** tab. Choose a programme, paste a test email (your own),
  and click **Pre-register & send activation**.
- Open that email → **set a password** → log in.
- **Expect:** that client sees and can book **only** the chosen programme.
- _(Use your own test email — this creates a real account and sends a real email.)_

### 3. Fewer, tidier tabs
- Look at the top menu.
- **Expect:** neatly grouped menus (no long row of tabs); every page still reachable.

### 4. No booking within 24 hours
- As a client, try to book a session that starts in less than 24 hours.
- **Expect:** those times don't show / are refused with a clear message.

### 5. Booking confirmation + Add to calendar
- Book a session.
- **Expect:** a confirmation email with an **Add to Google / Outlook / Apple**
  calendar option, plus an **Add to Calendar** button on the session card in the app.

### 6. Join button stays active until the session ends
- On a session that has started, look at the **Join** button.
- **Expect:** it stays clickable throughout the session (until the end + a short
  grace), for both you and the client.

### 7. In-session AI summary  *(use Chrome or Edge, two people)*
- Start a real session, talk for a minute, then end it.
- **Expect:** shortly after, an **AI Summary** button appears on the completed
  session card, showing a summary, key points and action items.
- You'll see an **"AI note-taking is on"** notice during the call, with a toggle
  to turn it off.

### 8. Logo goes to the home page
- While signed in, click the **Dr. Nath logo**.
- **Expect:** it opens the home page (you stay logged in).

### 9. Post-session notes / action items
- On a completed session (client view), click **Add Notes**.
- **Expect:** you can save key takeaways and check off action items; the coach can
  view the client's notes on their side.

### 10. Dashboard analytics  — *your input needed*
- Admin → **Analytics**.
- **Expect:** retention, coachees by company, top coaches, habit consistency,
  sessions and revenue.
- **Please tell me:** which **specific metrics/numbers** you most want to track,
  and I'll add them.

If any item doesn't match what's described, note the number and send it over —
I'll fix it right away.
