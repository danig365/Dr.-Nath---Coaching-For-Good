# Additional Features Requested — Beyond the Original Brief

This document lists the requests from your feedback that go **beyond the scope agreed in the original project brief** (*The Brief: Coaching for Good — C4G*). Everything below is buildable and we're glad to take it on — but because each is a new addition rather than a fix to existing work, they require a **separate budget**.

For fairness, every item notes **why** it sits outside the original brief, with a reference to the relevant brief section.

> Everything from your feedback that *was* covered by the brief — the session-outcome fix, the AI summary emails (which also serve as the written **report of each discussion and its key points** you asked for), the reminder system, and the name and numbering corrections — is **already done at no extra cost**. See *What's Fixed & How to Check It* for those.

> **On "making the platform more AI-compliant / integrating AI into everything" (feedback point 3):** this is a broad direction rather than a single feature. Part of it is already delivered — the automatic AI summary and report after each session. The remaining concrete pieces are items **1, 2 and 3** below, which is where the additional AI work is quoted.

---

## 1. Advanced meeting analytics (Zoom-style)
**What it is:** A scored report for each session — a **Meeting Score**, **Engagement** level and **Sentiment** (mood) reading; a **downloadable full transcript** of every meeting; and a **deep-dive** view with indicators such as Participant Score, Engagement, Sentiment, Charisma and Bias, each with a plain-English explanation.

**Why it's additional:** The brief's Analytics section (4.8) covers **business** reporting only — revenue, retention, active clients, coaching hours, best-performing coach. Per-meeting scoring, sentiment analysis and transcript downloads are not part of it. Video *recording* is in the brief (4.3), but the analysis and scoring layer on top of it is new.

---

## 2. Weekly AI insights for the coach
**What it is:** An automatic weekly email to you with (a) a list of **clients you haven't interacted with recently** so no one slips through the cracks, and (b) a summary of the **most-discussed topics** across your sessions.

**Why it's additional:** The brief provides dashboards you open yourself (4.8); it does not include an AI that proactively watches your client activity and emails you weekly prompts. This is a new AI capability.

---

## 3. AI habit-coaching assistant
**What it is:** An AI tool that helps clients **build and sustain healthy habits** — nutrition, physical activity, sleep, stress, mindfulness, relationships, burnout and work-life balance — both during and between sessions (suggestions, nudges and check-ins driven by AI).

**Why it's additional:** The brief includes a **manual Habit Tracker** (4.4) — clients logging daily check-ins themselves. An AI layer that interprets those habits and actively coaches the client is a substantial new capability beyond a tracker.

---

## 4. Canvas-style learning space
**What it is:** Aligning the platform with a Canvas-like (LMS) structure — a **dedicated landing page for each programme**, **announcements**, and a **shared space** for documents and video uploads per programme.

**Why it's additional:** The brief includes a general Resource Library, calendar and mailing tools, but **no Canvas / LMS integration** and no per-programme course pages or announcements. This is a new module.

---

## 5. Free "Chemistry Session" (discovery call)
**What it is:** A new public option — **"Book a Chemistry Session with Dr Nath"** / "Book a Call" — letting a prospective client book a **free** introductory call before committing to a paid programme.

**Why it's additional:** The brief's Booking Engine (4.2) covers booking paid sessions and packages. A separate free discovery-call offering, presented publicly as its own flow, is a new addition.

---

## 6. Intake-form-gated booking flow
**What it is:** For the chemistry session above — a visitor first completes an **Intake Form**, and only after submitting it does your calendar unlock so they can book. Their answers are attached to the booking so you can read them before the call.

**Why it's additional:** Basic intake forms exist in the brief (4.7, Template Builder), but they are sent by a coach to an *existing* client. A **public, pre-registration intake form that gates access to the calendar** is a new flow that has to be built from scratch.

> **On the current flow you asked about** ("Book a Session" → register → *then what?*): today, after a visitor registers they are taken straight to the booking page to choose a programme and time. The change you're describing replaces that "register first" step with "intake form first, then book" for the free chemistry call.

> **One decision needed before we start #5 and #6:** should a chemistry-session visitor create an account, or book as a guest/lead with no account? This affects the build, so we'll confirm your preference (and the intake-form questions) first.

---

## Not a development item — for discussion
**The C4G mobile application & payment (feedback point 6).** Starting the mobile app and the payment schedule (upfront / balance) is a **commercial** matter, not a build task in itself. The native mobile app *is* part of the original brief, so it is a **contracted next phase**, not an out-of-scope extra — it simply needs to be scheduled and its payment milestone agreed.
