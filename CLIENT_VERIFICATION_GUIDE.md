# Dr. Nath Platform — What's Fixed & How to Check It

A simple, running list of what has been fixed and exactly how you can confirm each one yourself.

> **Before you start:** please open the site and press **Ctrl + Shift + R** once (this loads the newest version). Sign in as usual.

---

## 1. Your email reminders now come to you ✅
**What was wrong:** reminders were going to your platform address, not your real inbox.
**Fixed:** they now come to **nathinno@gmail.com** — session reminders, cancellations, everything.
**How to check:**
- Look in **nathinno@gmail.com** for reminder emails before a session.
- *(If you don't see one, check the Spam/Junk folder once — Gmail can be cautious at first.)*

---

## 2. You can now edit your whole profile ✅
**What was wrong:** only your name and bio could be edited.
**Fixed:** you can now edit your **email, LinkedIn, specialties, certifications, rate, experience, languages and industries**. Clients can edit their own details too.
**How to check:**
- Go to your **Profile** → click **Edit Profile** → change any field → **Save**.
- Refresh the page — your change should still be there.

---

## 3. Session numbering is now correct (1st, 2nd, 3rd…) ✅
**What was wrong:** sessions that were cancelled or didn't happen were still counted, so the numbers were too high.
**Fixed:** only sessions that **actually took place** (or are still upcoming) are counted.
**How to check** (in **My Sessions**):
- **Sandra Holloway – 25 July** now reads **1st session** (was showing 2nd).
- **Lucy – 18 July** now reads **2nd session** (was showing 3rd).

---

## 4. You can now correct a session's outcome yourself ✅  *(this is the big one)*
**What was wrong:** the platform guessed "Completed" or "No Show" automatically, which was sometimes wrong — a session that ran on WhatsApp was flagged "No Show", and one that never happened showed "Completed".
**Fixed:** you can now set the true outcome on any finished session yourself.
**How to check** (in **My Sessions**, on any past or No-Show session):
- Click **Correct outcome** and choose one of:
  - **Took place — on the platform**
  - **Took place — off the platform (e.g. WhatsApp)**
  - **No show — someone didn't join**
  - **Did not take place at all**
- The badge and the session number update immediately.
- *(Already done for you: Sandra's 4 July is now "Did not take place"; her 25 July shows as her real 1st session.)*

## 5. Duplicate names fixed ✅
**What was wrong:** names showed twice — "Heidi Heidi", "Lucy Lucy".
**Fixed:** they now read **"Heidi"** and **"Lucy"**.
**How to check:** open **My Sessions** — each name now appears once.

## 6. Automatic "let's reschedule" email ✅
**What was wrong:** when a session was missed or cancelled, no one was told.
**Fixed:** if a session is **cancelled or missed**, you and the client both get an email — with a button for the client to book a new time.
**How to check:** cancel a test session (or wait for a missed one) — a "Let's reschedule" email arrives at **nathinno@gmail.com** and to the client.

## 7. Call audio — echo / "radio-like" noise 🟡 *(please test)*
**What we've done:** the call audio has been tuned for clear speech, and we've added an on-screen reminder to use headphones, plus a note that turning AI note-taking off can help if the noise appears.

**Note on the "server":** the call server is chosen **automatically** based on each person's location — there's nothing to set up on that side. The audio issues are best solved from the call itself.

**On your next session, please try these in order:**
1. **Use headphones or earphones** — both you *and* the client. This is the single biggest fix: speakers let each side's sound loop back into the microphone, which causes the echo and "radio-like" noise.
2. **If the noise is still there, turn off AI note-taking for that call** — tap the AI icon during the meeting to switch it off. (It listens to your microphone, which can interfere with the audio on some computers.)
3. **Use a strong internet connection** — sit close to your WiFi router, or use a cable if you can. Ask the client to do the same.
4. **Use Google Chrome** (updated to the latest version).
5. **Close other heavy apps and browser tabs** during the call, so the computer has enough power for clear audio.
6. **Only one device per person** in the call — don't join from a laptop and a phone at the same time.

Please tell us **which one helped** — that tells us exactly what's causing it, so we can remove it for good.

## 8. AI session summary now emailed to both of you ✅  *(new)*
**What was wrong:** the AI summary was only stored on the platform — you had to log in to see it, and the client never received it.
**Fixed:** as soon as the AI summary is ready (within minutes of the session ending), it's **emailed automatically to both you and the client** — with the overview, key points and action items — plus a **"Book your next session now"** button.
**How to check:**
- Hold a session on the platform with the AI note-taking icon **on**.
- After the call ends, look in **nathinno@gmail.com** (and the client's inbox) for a **"Your session summary"** email.
- It should show the summary + a gold **Book your next session now** button.
- The email also includes **Key points**, **Action items** and **Points to reflect on** (open questions for the client before the next session).
- The **client's** copy has a second button, **"Send me a reminder to schedule my next session"** — if the client clicks it, they'll get gentle nudges to rebook after **3, 5 and 7 days**. (Your coach copy doesn't need this button.)
- *Smart stop:* the moment the client books their next session, those reminders **stop automatically**.
- *If the client still hasn't rebooked* after all the reminders, **you (the coach) get an email** so you can reach out to them personally.
- *To test the reminder button:* click it in the client's email → a "You're all set" page appears, and the reminders begin.
- **This is also the written "report of the discussion and key points" you asked for** — you now receive it by email after every session, without needing to log in.

**What was wrong:** a new user could be set up as the wrong type (a client became a "coach") without noticing.

**How to check:** open the **Register** page — no role is ticked by default; you can't continue until you pick one.

## 10. Monthly newsletter — ready to preview & send ✅  *(new)*
**What this is:** you can now write, **preview** and send a newsletter to your subscribers, right from the dashboard. Your first issue — *"One month in — celebrating your wellness journey"* — is already prepared as a draft.
**How to check** (using the **admin dashboard** login):

- Scroll to **Issues** and find the draft.
