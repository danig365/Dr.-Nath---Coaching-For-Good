# Dr. Nath Platform — What's Fixed & How to Check It

A quick checklist of what's been fixed and how to confirm each one.

> **Before you start:** open the site and press **Ctrl + Shift + R** once (loads the newest version), then sign in.

---

### 1. Email reminders now reach you ✅
Reminders (and cancellations) now come to **nathinno@gmail.com**, not your platform address.
**Check:** look for a reminder email before a session. *(If missing, check Spam once.)*

### 2. Your whole profile is editable ✅
You can now edit email, LinkedIn, specialties, certifications, rate, experience, languages and industries. Clients can edit their details too.
**Check:** **Profile → Edit Profile →** change a field **→ Save →** refresh; it stays.

### 3. Session numbering is correct ✅
Only sessions that actually happened (or are upcoming) are counted.
**Check (My Sessions):** Sandra – 25 July shows **1st**; Lucy – 18 July shows **2nd**.

### 4. You can correct a session's outcome yourself ✅ *(the main one)*
The platform's automatic "Completed / No Show" was sometimes wrong. You can now set the true outcome.
**Check (My Sessions → any past session):** click **Correct outcome** → choose **On the platform / Off the platform (WhatsApp) / No show / Did not take place**. The badge and number update at once.
*(Already set for you: Sandra's 4 July = "Did not take place".)*

### 5. Duplicate names fixed ✅
"Heidi Heidi" and "Lucy Lucy" now read **Heidi** and **Lucy**.
**Check:** open **My Sessions** — each name appears once.

### 6. Automatic "let's reschedule" email ✅
If a session is cancelled or missed, you and the client both get an email with a re-booking button.
**Check:** cancel a test session — the email arrives at **nathinno@gmail.com** and to the client.

### 7. Call audio — breaking & "radio-like" noise ✅ *(please confirm on your next call)*
**The cause we found:** the AI note-taking and the call were both using your microphone at the same time, and on some calls they clashed — causing the audio to break up, crackle and echo. That's why it only happened on *some* sessions.
**Fixed:** the call now takes and keeps control of the microphone first, so that clash can no longer happen. We also improved voice clarity and auto-pause note-taking when your internet dips.
**New safety net:** every call now quietly records its own technical health, so if any audio trouble ever happens again, **we can look at exactly what went wrong and fix it precisely — no more guessing.**
**Please still:** use **headphones** (you and the client) for the clearest sound. If you ever hear trouble again, just tell us the **date and time** and we'll check the logs.

### 8. AI summary now emailed to both of you ✅
Within minutes of a session ending, the AI summary is emailed to **you and the client** — overview, key points, action items and reflection points, plus a **Book your next session** button. *(This is also the written report of each discussion you asked for — no need to log in.)*
The client's copy also has **"Send me a reminder"** → gentle nudges at 3, 5 and 7 days; these **stop automatically** once they rebook, and if they still don't, **you get an alert** to reach out.
**Check:** hold a session with AI note-taking **on**; after it ends, look for a **"Your session summary"** email in **nathinno@gmail.com** and the client's inbox.

### 9. Sign-up asks for the correct role ✅
New users must clearly pick **Client** or **Coach** — nothing is pre-selected.
**Check:** open **Register** — no role is ticked; you can't continue until you choose.

### 10. Monthly newsletter — ready to preview & send ✅
You can write, **preview** and send newsletters from the dashboard. Your first issue, *"One month in — celebrating your wellness journey,"* is prepared as a draft.
**Check (admin login):** **Activity → Newsletter → Issues →** click **Preview** to see it exactly as it lands in the inbox, then **Send** when happy. *(It goes to newsletter subscribers — tell us if you'd like your clients added.)*

---

*If anything doesn't behave as described, just note the item number and what you saw, and it'll be sorted.*
